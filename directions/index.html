Travel Guide: Manila Airport to De La Salle University (DLSU)

Step 1: Arrival at Manila Airport

  • Choose Your Transport: Use Grab (ride-hailing app) or a metered taxi to leave the airport.

Step 2: Head to De La Salle University

De La Salle University is located along Taft Avenue in Manila, approximately 8–10 kilometers from NAIA. Depending on traffic, the trip may take 20–40 minutes. Here are your main options:

Option 1: Grab (Recommended)
  • Open the Grab app, set your destination to "De La Salle University - Taft," and book a ride.
  • Estimated Cost: ₱200–₱350, depending on traffic and demand.
Option 2: Taxi
  • Take a metered taxi from the airport taxi stand.
  • Fare: Approximately ₱200–₱300, depending on traffic.
Option 3: Public Transport (Budget-Friendly)
  • Option A: Airport Bus to EDSA-Taft:
    • Take the UBE Express or the P2P bus to EDSA-Taft (cost: ₱50–₱100).
    • From EDSA-Taft, take the LRT Line 1 train southbound to Vito Cruz Station (₱20).
    • Walk to DLSU (approximately 5 minutes).
  • Option B: Jeepney: After reaching EDSA-Taft, take a jeepney bound for Taft Avenue/Vito Cruz and ask to be dropped near DLSU (fare: ₱15–₱20).

Step 3: Arrival at De La Salle University

  • Landmark: DLSU is along Taft Avenue, across from Rizal Memorial Sports Complex and near the Vito Cruz LRT Station.
  • Main Entrance: The gates are typically located at South Gate (entry towards Henry Sy Hall).

Additional Tips

  • Peak Hours: Traffic in Manila can be heavy during rush hours (7–10 AM, 4–8 PM).
  • Stay Connected: Use mobile data or connect to airport Wi-Fi to book a Grab ride or check LRT schedules.
