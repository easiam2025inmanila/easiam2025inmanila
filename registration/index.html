Satellite Meeting Proposal Submission

The Organizing Committee aims to create a high-quality scientific program, with Satellite Meetings on July 4, 2025, enhancing the main program by addressing timely topics in Industrial and Applied Mathematics.

We encourage you to contribute by submitting a proposal for a Satellite Meeting on any topic related to Industrial and Applied Mathematics. The following guidelines outline the procedures for submitting Satellite Meeting proposals.

  • •  The Satellite Meeting title.
  • •  Type of meeting: Workshop, Networking Reception, Training Session, etc.
  • •  Organizers' names and affiliations (max. 2): At least one organizer must serve as chair(s) of the meeting.
  • •  Summary of the proposed activity: Specify its goals and broad appeal to participants of EASIAM2025.
  • •  The structure of the meeting is free, with the only limitation of the time slot scheduled for it.
  • •  Confirmation from the proposed speakers (e.g., email or verbal acceptance).
  • •  The meeting can be conducted in a language other than English if appropriately justified.
  • •  We strongly recommend organizers to create a simple webpage (e.g., Google Site) for the meeting.

To submit a proposal, please fill out the Google Form

If you are unable to access the Google Form and would like to submit a satellite meeting, please email easiam2025inmanila[at]gmail.com, ensuring that the details mentioned above are included. Additionally, kindly specify in the subject line that your submission is categorized as [SATELLITE], followed by the title of your satellite meeting.

All approved Satellite Meetings will be held on July 4, 2025, at De La Salle University.
