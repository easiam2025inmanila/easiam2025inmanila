Refund Announcement

For participants who already paid the registration fee, if you are unable to attend to the conference, the following refund policy is in place:

  • Full refund can be issued to your original account if requested before June 1, 2025.
  • 50% refund can be issued to your original account if requested between June 2, 2025 and June 15, 2025.
  • No refund can be issued on or after June 16, 2025.

Note: Any charges incurred during the refund process will be covered by the requester.
